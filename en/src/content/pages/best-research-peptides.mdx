# Best Research Peptides

Research peptides are selected based on the biological system being studied. This guide highlights the most commonly studied research peptides across the main classes.

## What Makes a Research Peptide Popular

Research peptides are widely studied when they offer a useful tool for investigating a specific biological pathway. Popular research peptides are typically well-documented, stable and relevant to active areas of investigation.

The most commonly studied research peptides are grouped by the biological system they are used to study:

- **GLP-1 receptor agonists** — semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide
- **Recovery peptides** — BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu
- **Longevity peptides** — NAD+, epitalon, MOTS-c
- **Growth hormone peptides** — HGH, ipamorelin, CJC-1295

## GLP-1 Receptor Agonists

GLP-1 receptor agonists are studied for their effects on glucose metabolism and incretin signaling. Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist, tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 agonist, and retatrutide is a triple receptor agonist. These compounds are used to investigate metabolic regulation in experimental models.

## Recovery Peptides

Recovery peptides are studied for their potential roles in tissue repair and regeneration. BPC-157 and TB-500 are studied in tissue repair research, while GHK-Cu is studied in dermatological and extracellular matrix research. These compounds are used to investigate regenerative processes.

## Longevity Peptides

Longevity peptides are studied in the context of aging and cellular health. NAD+ is a coenzyme studied in cellular metabolism, epitalon is studied in biological rhythm research, and MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-derived peptide studied in metabolic research.

## Growth Hormone Peptides

Growth hormone peptides are studied in endocrine and growth research. HGH is a polypeptide hormone, while ipamorelin and CJC-1295 are growth hormone secretagogues. These compounds are used to investigate the growth hormone axis.
