# Compounded Tirzepatide

Compounded tirzepatide is a topic of growing interest in the context of GLP-1 and incretin research. This page explains what compounded tirzepatide is and how it relates to research peptides.

## What Is Compounded Tirzepatide?

Compounded tirzepatide refers to tirzepatide that has been prepared by a compounding pharmacy, typically to provide a customized formulation. In a research context, tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist studied for its effects on metabolic regulation.

Tirzepatide is a synthetic peptide that acts as an agonist at both the glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) receptor and the glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor. It is a 39-amino-acid peptide with a structure that incorporates a fatty-acid side chain.

## How Tirzepatide Works in Research

In laboratory models, tirzepatide binds to both the GIP and GLP-1 receptors, activating distinct but complementary signaling pathways. GLP-1 receptor activation supports glucose-dependent insulin secretion, while GIP receptor activation is studied for its role in lipid metabolism and incretin signaling.
