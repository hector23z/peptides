## What Is Tirzepatide?

Tirzepatide is a synthetic peptide that acts as an agonist at both the glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) receptor and the glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor. It is a 39-amino-acid peptide with a structure that incorporates a fatty-acid side chain, which contributes to its extended stability profile.

In laboratory research, tirzepatide is used to investigate dual-receptor signaling, glucose regulation and energy metabolism. Its dual activity makes it a subject of interest in metabolic research, where investigators study the combined effects of incretin pathway activation.

The price of research-grade tirzepatide also reflects the complexity of its synthesis. As a dual-receptor agonist with a modified structure, tirzepatide requires careful synthesis and purification, which contributes to its cost relative to simpler peptides.
