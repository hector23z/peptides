# Peptide Shop

A research peptide shop provides high-purity peptides for laboratory investigation. This page explains what to expect when shopping for research-grade peptides.

## What a Research Peptide Shop Offers

A research peptide shop supplies lyophilized peptides across multiple classes, each used to study a distinct area of biology:

- **GLP-1 receptor agonists** — semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide
- **Recovery peptides** — BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, melanotan-2, PT-141
- **Longevity peptides** — NAD+, epitalon, MOTS-c
- **Growth hormone peptides** — HGH, ipamorelin, CJC-1295

Each product is supplied as a lyophilized powder for reconstitution in research protocols.

## What to Look For When Shopping

When shopping for research peptides, prioritize quality and documentation. Look for:

- **High purity** — verified by HPLC, typically above 99%
- **Certificates of analysis** — documentation of purity and peptide content
- **Accurate product information** — clear descriptions of size and handling
- **Reliable quality control** — consistent results across batches

## How to Evaluate a Peptide Shop

A reputable peptide shop should provide transparent documentation and responsive support. Look for shops that offer detailed certificates of analysis and clear product information. This transparency helps researchers verify that the material matches its specifications.

When evaluating a shop, consider the range of products available, the quality of the documentation provided and the responsiveness of customer support. A shop that is transparent about its quality control processes is more likely to deliver reliable material.

## How Research Peptides Are Supplied

Research peptides are supplied as lyophilized powders that are stable during shipping and storage. They are reconstituted with an appropriate solvent before use. Proper storage, in a cool, dry place protected from light and moisture, helps maintain quality.

## Reconstitution and Handling

Before use, lyophilized peptides must be reconstituted with an appropriate solvent, such as bacteriostatic water or sterile water. The reconstitution protocol should follow published literature and institutional guidelines. Gentle handling and proper storage help preserve peptide integrity.

## Handling & Storage

Proper handling and storage are essential for maintaining peptide quality. Lyophilized powders should be stored in a cool, dry place, protected from light and moisture. Reconstituted material should be refrigerated and used within the timeframe specified by the research protocol. Avoiding repeated freeze-thaw cycles helps preserve peptide stability and reduces the risk of degradation.
