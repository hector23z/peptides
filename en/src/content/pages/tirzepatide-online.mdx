## What Is Tirzepatide?

Tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist studied in laboratory research settings. It is a synthetic peptide that acts as an agonist at both the glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) receptor and the glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor.

In laboratory research, tirzepatide is used to investigate dual-receptor signaling, glucose regulation and energy metabolism. It is supplied as a lyophilized powder for reconstitution in research protocols.
