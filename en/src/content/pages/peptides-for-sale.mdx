## Evaluating Quality

Quality is the most important consideration when buying research peptides. Look for suppliers that provide independently verified purity testing and certificates of analysis. High-purity material reduces the risk of impurities affecting experimental results.

Key quality indicators include:

- HPLC purity above 99%
- Documented peptide content and molecular weight
- Consistent batch quality control
- Transparent handling and storage practices

## How Research Peptides Are Supplied

Research peptides are supplied as lyophilized powders that are stable during shipping and storage. They are reconstituted with an appropriate solvent before use. Proper storage, in a cool, dry place protected from light and moisture, helps maintain quality.

The lyophilized form is designed for stability and convenience. It allows researchers to store material for extended periods and to prepare solutions at the concentration required for their specific protocol.

## Reconstitution and Handling

Before use, lyophilized peptides must be reconstituted with an appropriate solvent, such as bacteriostatic water or sterile water. The reconstitution protocol should follow published literature and institutional guidelines. Gentle handling and proper storage help preserve peptide integrity.

After reconstitution, the material should be stored according to the research protocol, typically refrigerated. Avoiding repeated freeze-thaw cycles helps maintain peptide stability.

When comparing suppliers, it is also worth considering the consistency of their quality control. A supplier that tests every batch and provides detailed documentation demonstrates a commitment to quality that supports reliable research.

## Research Use Only

Research peptides are intended for laboratory research purposes only. They are not for human or veterinary consumption, and they are not approved by any regulatory authority for medical use. By purchasing research peptides, you confirm that the material will be used solely for research purposes.
