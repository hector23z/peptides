## What Is Testosterone?

Testosterone is a steroid hormone that plays a central role in the development and maintenance of male reproductive tissues and secondary sexual characteristics. It is studied in laboratory research for its effects on growth, metabolism and endocrine signaling.

In laboratory research, testosterone is used to investigate androgen receptor signaling, hormonal regulation and related physiological processes. It is supplied as a research compound for use in experimental protocols.

## How Testosterone Works in Research

In laboratory models, testosterone binds to the androgen receptor, activating downstream signaling pathways that influence growth, metabolism and cellular function. Research focuses on the mechanisms through which androgens exert their effects.
