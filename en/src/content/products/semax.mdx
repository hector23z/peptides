## What Is the Chemical Structure of Semax?

Semax is the heptapeptide Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro (MEHFPGP), with a free-peptide molecular mass of approximately 813.9 g/mol. It combines the ACTH(4–7) sequence with a Pro-Gly-Pro extension; counterion and terminal form still require lot-level confirmation.

## How Can Semax Results Be Interpreted Without Overclaiming?

Interpret Semax with an evidence ladder: verified material, recovered exposure, proximal pathway response and only then a downstream phenotype. A change in migration, viability or gene expression does not by itself prove the proposed molecular target. Each step needs its own control. Sequence or structure controls test specificity; receptor blockade tests pathway dependence; viability and matrix controls test whether the assay itself explains the result.
