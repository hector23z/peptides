# Vitamin D3

Vitamin D3, also known as cholecalciferol, is a research compound studied in laboratory research. It is a fat-soluble vitamin that is studied in research on calcium metabolism and related physiological processes.

## Composition

Vitamin D3 is a secosteroid with a molecular weight of approximately 384.6 g/mol. It is a fat-soluble vitamin with a structure related to steroid hormones.
