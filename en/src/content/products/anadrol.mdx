## What Is Anadrol?

Anadrol is a synthetic steroid derived from dihydrotestosterone, with a methyl group at the 17-alpha position and a hydroxymethylene group at the 2-position. It is studied in research on androgen receptor signaling and related physiological processes.

In laboratory research, anadrol is used to investigate the mechanisms of androgen action, including receptor binding and downstream signaling.
