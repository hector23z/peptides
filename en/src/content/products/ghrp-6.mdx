## What Is the Chemical Structure of GHRP-6?

GHRP-6 is a synthetic hexapeptide with a molecular weight of approximately 873 g/mol. It is a growth hormone secretagogue that acts on the ghrelin receptor.

## What Does Current Research Establish About GHRP-6?

GHRP-6 is the hexapeptide His-D-Trp-Ala-Trp-D-Phe-Lys-NH2 and a GHSR1a agonist. It is often distinguished experimentally by stronger feeding-related responses than some later secretagogues, but receptor expression must be verified.

## How Can GHRP-6 Results Be Interpreted Without Overclaiming?

The strongest GHRP-6 evidence connects identity to mechanism to phenotype in that order. For GHRP-6, chemical identity, target engagement and downstream phenotype are separate layers. A change in migration, viability or gene expression does not by itself prove the proposed molecular target. A useful study includes an orthogonal analytical identity check, a target-dependent perturbation and a second assay that measures the same biology by a different method. This design makes a positive result easier to reproduce and a negative result easier to interpret.

For citation-ready GHRP-6 reporting, provide raw concentration units, biological replicate counts, prespecified exclusions and the exact time point. Separate exploratory endpoints from confirmatory ones. If exposure was not measured, describe potency as nominal rather than intracellular or free concentration. Limit the conclusion to the model used; avoid converting a pathway observation into a claim about clinical benefit.
