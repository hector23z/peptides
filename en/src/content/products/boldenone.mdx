## What Is Boldenone?

Boldenone is a synthetic steroid related to testosterone, with a structure that includes an additional double bond. It is studied in research on androgen receptor signaling and related physiological processes.

In laboratory research, boldenone is used to investigate the mechanisms of androgen action, including receptor binding and downstream signaling.

## What Is the Chemical Structure of Boldenone?
