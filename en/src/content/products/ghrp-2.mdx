## What Is the Chemical Structure of GHRP-2?

GHRP-2 is a synthetic hexapeptide with a molecular weight of approximately 817 g/mol. It is a growth hormone secretagogue that acts on the ghrelin receptor.

Conclusions should stay at the level tested: in vitro activity is not evidence of organism-level efficacy, and an animal phenotype is not a human claim.
