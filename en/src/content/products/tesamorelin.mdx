## What Does Current Research Establish About Tesamorelin?

Tesamorelin is a stabilized 44-residue GHRH analogue with an N-terminal trans-3-hexenoyl group. The acyl modification reduces enzymatic degradation while preserving GHRH-receptor agonism.
