## What is HGH?

Human growth hormone (hGH, somatropin) is a 191-residue protein with two intramolecular disulfide bonds and a mass near 22 kDa. The “24 IU” designation describes the labelled biological activity or presentation size; it does not identify a different hGH sequence from a 10 IU presentation. Research comparisons should use calibrated potency and molarity, not vial label alone.
