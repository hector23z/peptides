## What is Epitalon?

Epitalon is a four-amino-acid peptide (Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly) that has been the subject of research interest in the context of biological rhythms and aging-related studies. It is studied for its potential effects on the regulation of circadian and other biological rhythms in experimental models.

The peptide is of interest in longevity research because of its potential influence on the pineal gland and the regulation of melatonin-related pathways. Researchers use epitalon to investigate biological rhythm regulation and related physiological processes.

The peptide is also studied for its potential effects on the regulation of melatonin-related pathways, which broadens its relevance to investigations of neuroendocrine signaling.
