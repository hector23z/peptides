## What Is Nandrolone Decanoate?

Nandrolone decanoate is a long-chain ester of the androgen nandrolone. The decanoate ester is attached to the nandrolone molecule to modify its release characteristics in biological systems. It is studied in research on androgen receptor signaling and related physiological processes.

In laboratory research, nandrolone decanoate is used to investigate the mechanisms of androgen action, including receptor binding and downstream signaling.

## What Is the Chemical Structure of Nandrolone Decanoate?

Nandrolone decanoate consists of the nandrolone molecule with a decanoate ester group attached at the 17-beta position. The decanoate ester is a ten-carbon chain.

The molecular weight of nandrolone decanoate is approximately 428.6 g/mol. The long ester chain adds considerable molecular weight.

For Nandrolone Decanoate, the ester chain changes hydrophobicity and the rate of enzymatic regeneration of the free steroid; it does not change the intrinsic receptor pharmacology of the hydrolysed parent molecule.
