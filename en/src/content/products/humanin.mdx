## How Can Humanin Results Be Interpreted Without Overclaiming?

Interpret Humanin with an evidence ladder: verified material, recovered exposure, proximal pathway response and only then a downstream phenotype. For Humanin, chemical identity, target engagement and downstream phenotype are separate layers. A change in migration, viability or gene expression does not by itself prove the proposed molecular target. Each step needs its own control. Sequence or structure controls test specificity; receptor blockade tests pathway dependence; viability and matrix controls test whether the assay itself explains the result.
