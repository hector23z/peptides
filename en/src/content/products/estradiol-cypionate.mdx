## What Is Estradiol Cypionate?

Estradiol cypionate is a long-chain ester of the estrogen estradiol. The cypionate ester is attached to the estradiol molecule to modify its release characteristics in biological systems. It is studied in research on estrogen receptor signaling and related physiological processes.

In laboratory research, estradiol cypionate is used to investigate the mechanisms of estrogen action, including receptor binding and downstream signaling.

## What Is the Chemical Structure of Estradiol Cypionate?

Estradiol cypionate consists of the estradiol molecule with a cypionate ester group attached at the 17-beta position. The cypionate ester is a cyclopentylpropionate chain.

The molecular weight of estradiol cypionate is approximately 396.6 g/mol. The ester group increases the molecular weight of the compound compared with the base estradiol molecule.

For Estradiol Cypionate, the ester chain changes hydrophobicity and the rate of enzymatic regeneration of the free steroid; it does not change the intrinsic receptor pharmacology of the hydrolysed parent molecule.
