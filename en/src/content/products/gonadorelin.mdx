## What Is the Chemical Structure of Gonadorelin?

Gonadorelin is a decapeptide with a molecular weight of approximately 1.2 kDa. Synthetic gonadorelin has the same ten-residue sequence as human GnRH; it is the native hormone sequence, not merely an unspecified analogue.
