## What is HGH?

HGH, also known as somatotropin, is a 191-amino-acid polypeptide hormone produced by the anterior pituitary gland. It is studied for its central role in growth, cellular metabolism and tissue development in experimental models.

The hormone is of interest in research on growth regulation, metabolic processes and the endocrine system. Researchers use HGH to investigate the physiological roles of growth hormone signaling in laboratory settings.

The hormone is also studied for its potential effects on cellular proliferation and differentiation, which broadens its relevance to investigations of tissue development.

The hormone is also studied for its potential role in the regulation of insulin-like growth factors, which broadens its relevance to investigations of the growth hormone axis.
