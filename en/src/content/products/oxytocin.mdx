## What Is the Chemical Structure of Oxytocin?

Oxytocin is the amidated nonapeptide Cys-Tyr-Ile-Gln-Asn-Cys-Pro-Leu-Gly-NH2 with an intramolecular Cys1–Cys6 disulfide bond. The oxidized free peptide has a molecular mass of approximately 1007.2 g/mol. Reduction, terminal deamidation or a different counterion changes the analytical record.

## What Does Current Research Establish About Oxytocin?

Oxytocin is a cyclic nonapeptide with a Cys1–Cys6 disulfide and amidated C-terminus. Vasopressin-receptor cross-reactivity emerges with concentration, making receptor-selective controls essential.

A citable Oxytocin evidence unit identifies the model, concentration, comparator, target-proximal readout and analytical identity.
