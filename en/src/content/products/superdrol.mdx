## What Is Superdrol?

Superdrol is a synthetic steroid derived from dihydrotestosterone, with methyl groups at the 2-alpha and 17-alpha positions. It is studied in research on androgen receptor signaling and related physiological processes.

In laboratory research, superdrol is used to investigate the mechanisms of androgen action, including receptor binding and downstream signaling.

## What Is the Chemical Structure of Superdrol?

Superdrol is structurally derived from dihydrotestosterone, with methyl groups at the 2-alpha and 17-alpha positions. These structural modifications distinguish it from the base androgen.

The molecular weight of superdrol is approximately 332.5 g/mol. The structural modifications are relevant to studies of structure-activity relationships.

The structural relationship between superdrol and dihydrotestosterone is relevant to comparative studies of androgen structure and function.

Superdrol is among the more studied dihydrotestosterone derivatives in research, and its distinct structure makes it a useful tool for investigating androgen receptor selectivity.
