## What Is the Chemical Structure of AHK-Cu?

AHK-Cu is a copper(II) complex of Ala-His-Lys. The free tripeptide is approximately 354.4 Da; a nominal 1:1 complex is approximately 415.9 Da before counterions and hydration are considered. Copper occupancy, coordination state and free copper must therefore be reported instead of assigning the complex an unsupported single value near 401 Da.
