## What Is the Chemical Structure of SS-31?

SS-31 is a small synthetic peptide with a molecular weight of approximately 639.8 g/mol. It is designed to interact with cardiolipin in the inner mitochondrial membrane, which is relevant to studies of mitochondrial function.

## What Does Current Research Establish About SS-31?

SS-31 (elamipretide) is the aromatic-cationic tetrapeptide D-Arg-Dmt-Lys-Phe-NH2. It partitions to the inner mitochondrial membrane and interacts with cardiolipin; mitochondrial uptake should not be described as membrane-potential driven without testing.

## How Can SS-31 Results Be Interpreted Without Overclaiming?

Begin interpretation with a falsifiable question and a predeclared primary readout. For SS-31, chemical identity, target engagement and downstream phenotype are separate layers. A change in migration, viability or gene expression does not by itself prove the proposed molecular target. Report negative and null findings, not only the largest response. Show individual observations, biological replicates and uncertainty intervals, and identify whether replication means a new well, a new culture, a new animal or a new day.

A citable SS-31 result states the species, cell line or biochemical system; exact molecular form; concentration and exposure; comparator; assay method; and statistical unit. Confirm that vehicle, pH, osmolality or precipitation did not create the phenotype. Conclusions should stay at the level tested: in vitro activity is not evidence of organism-level efficacy, and an animal phenotype is not a human claim.
