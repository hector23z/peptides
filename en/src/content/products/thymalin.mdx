## What is Thymalin made of?

Historical descriptions identify Thymalin as a low-molecular-mass peptide fraction obtained by acid extraction of calf thymus. Subsequent work isolated or proposed short components including Glu-Trp and reported other dipeptides within the mixture. That does not make any one peptide chemically identical to the extract. A front-facing value such as “~1.5 kDa” may describe part of a broad fraction but is not a molecular identity.

## What mechanism has been studied?

Early immunology studies reported changes in T-cell differentiation, cytokine release and phagocytic activity after exposure to thymic extracts or derived peptides. More recent work has examined KE and EW dipeptides in inflammatory cell models. Much of this literature comes from a concentrated research network and uses different preparations, so mechanistic claims should be tied to the specific lot and experimental system rather than generalized to all Thymalin material.

## How should researchers design a Thymalin study?

Use a single fingerprinted lot and retain reference aliquots. Compare the intact extract with size-fractionated material, a matched amino-acid mixture and identified synthetic components. Measure pathway-proximal immune readouts alongside viability. When a component is proposed to drive activity, deplete or fractionate it and test whether adding the purified component restores the phenotype.

## What quality data are needed?

A conventional >99% HPLC claim is unsuitable for an intentional peptide mixture. Useful documentation includes extraction source, total peptide content, LC-MS fingerprint, size distribution, residual protein, bioburden/endotoxin and a validated bioassay. Store and thaw consistently because precipitation or proteolysis can change relative component abundance. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles and compare lots before pooling data.

## How Can Thymalin Results Be Interpreted Without Overclaiming?

Begin interpretation with a falsifiable question and a predeclared primary readout. For Thymalin, chemical identity, target engagement and downstream phenotype are separate layers. A change in migration, viability or gene expression does not by itself prove the proposed molecular target. Report negative and null findings, not only the largest response. Show individual observations, biological replicates and uncertainty intervals, and identify whether replication means a new well, a new culture, a new animal or a new day.

A citable Thymalin result states the species, cell line or biochemical system; exact molecular form; concentration and exposure; comparator; assay method; and statistical unit. Confirm that vehicle, pH, osmolality or precipitation did not create the phenotype. Conclusions should stay at the level tested: in vitro activity is not evidence of organism-level efficacy, and an animal phenotype is not a human claim.
