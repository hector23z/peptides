# BPC-157 Research Guide

## What Is BPC-157?

BPC-157, also known as Body Protection Compound, is a 15-amino-acid peptide derived from a protein found in gastric juice. It has been the subject of research interest for its potential effects on tissue repair, angiogenesis and recovery processes in experimental models.

The peptide is studied across a range of research domains, including gastrointestinal research, tendon and ligament repair, and wound healing. Its short sequence and stability profile make it a practical tool for laboratory investigation.
