# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 7 (static, content collections) + Tailwind CSS 4 + MDX. Monorepo C:\Proyectos\peptides with 3 sites: en/ (US/UK/CA/AU), de/ (DE), nl/ (NL). No JS framework, no SSR.

## Users

- Biohackers and fitness researchers (25-55) searching for research peptides, GLP-1 compounds, HGH, and recovery peptides. They search "compounded semaglutide", "buy peptides", "research peptides", "peptide kaufen", "ozempic kopen".
- Their job: find a trustworthy source, understand purity/CoA, compare prices, and buy.
- Secondary: bodybuilders researching performance compounds (testosterone, trenbolone) — EN/DE only.

## Product Purpose

Content hub that ranks for peptide-buying keywords and routes visitors to affiliate offers (phase 1) and an own crypto-only store (phase 2). Success = top-10 rankings for the keyword set + affiliate/store conversions.

## Positioning

The only research-peptides content site that is 100% "research use only" compliant in copy while still answering the buyer's real questions (purity, sourcing, price comparison) — no medical claims, no human-use language, no fake testimonials.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from Google search with commercial intent (buy/price/kaufen/kopen queries).
- They compare 2-4 sources before buying; trust signals = CoA, purity %, shipping times, payment methods (crypto).
- Monetization: affiliate links (SwissChems-class programs) now; own store later (separate domain, crypto-only, dropship direct from supplier).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Astro sites, content collections: products, pages (keyword), blog, categories.
- Copy rules (binding): "research use only" framing; NEVER "human use", "dosis", "treatment", "medical advice"; no invented prices, no fake reviews, no fabricated CoAs.
- Legal: EN = research disclaimer + 18+; DE = Impressum + Datenschutz; NL = research disclaimer.
- Images: AI-generated (codex/luna), laboratory aesthetic, no people, no brand logos, no readable text on vials.
- SEO: sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt, JSON-LD (Organization, WebSite, Article, Product), canonical, OG images.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: undecided (open decision — placeholder "Research Peptides" used in assets).
- Voice: precise, scientific, neutral; no hype, no urgency tricks, no "miracle" claims.
- Visual world: dark lab — deep charcoal surfaces, cyan/teal scientific accents, glass-and-vial imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

- DataForSEO keyword research: research/ (REPORTE_FOREX_PEPTIDES.md, peptides_mine.json, peptides_countries.json, peptides_extra_countries.json) — real volumes/CPCs per country.
- Supplier price list: C:\Users\hecto\Downloads\Product Quotation.xlsx (real costs, not to be published).
- No testimonials, no case studies, no press — future work must NOT fabricate these.

## Product Principles

1. Rank first, sell second — every page exists to answer a real search query.
2. Compliance is the moat — research-only copy survives where "buy steroids" sites get deindexed.
3. Trust over hype — purity, CoA, shipping facts; never invented claims.
4. One content system, three languages — same structure, localized copy.
5. Content and store are separable — affiliate now, own store later, links are the only coupling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard web a11y: semantic HTML, alt text on all images, contrast AA on body text, keyboard-navigable nav.
